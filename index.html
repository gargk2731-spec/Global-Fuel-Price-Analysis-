About the project

A data-led view of fuel prices

This project uses a multi-country dataset to explore fuel-price measures and taxation from 2020 through 2026.

Compare prices, follow trends, and inspect regional differences.

The Tableau workbook brings together summary measures, price distributions, time-series views, regional tax comparisons and country-level rankings. The dashboard supports exploration, while the story presents a focused comparison of the top three countries under the selected metric.

These charts show descriptive patterns in the supplied data. A visible association between taxation and prices does not, by itself, prove causation.

Questions explored

  • How do fuel prices change over time?
  • How do prices vary across countries and regions?
  • How does the recorded tax measure differ by region?
  • Which countries rank highest under the selected comparison metric?

FAQ

About the analysis

Quick notes on how to interpret this project.

01

What does the project cover?

It explores petrol, diesel and LPG price measures, the Brent crude measure and recorded taxation over 2020–2026.

02

Can I interact with the charts?

Yes. The embedded Tableau views provide the interaction enabled in the published workbook. You can also open each view directly on Tableau Public.

03

Does a tax-price relationship prove causation?

No. This is descriptive analysis; causal conclusions require additional evidence and an appropriate research design.

04

Why might a country ranking change?

The order can depend on the selected measure, date range, filters and aggregation.
